================================================================================

1. FAQ SECTION BACKGROUND (HomeLanding.tsx, line 337)

   BEFORE:
   -------
   <section id="faq" className="bg-gradient-to-b from-[var(--ink)] via-[#1a1a1a] to-[var(--ink)]">

   AFTER:
   ------
   <section id="faq" className="bg-gradient-to-b from-[var(--ink)] to-[var(--porcelain-2)]">

   EFFECT: Gradient now transitions from Veja's dark (ink) down to light (porcelain-2),
           matching FAQ item backgrounds and creating smooth, cohesive flow.

   .gradient-bridge-ink-to-porcelain
     → Use after dark hero to bridge to light content zone

   .gradient-bridge-ink-to-porcelain-2
     → Use to transition from dark anchor bands to light base (applied to FAQ)

   .gradient-bridge-porcelain-to-ink
     → Use to transition from light content to dark showcase/footer (used in Veja)
